BookVerse: book recommendation website

A small three-page site built during my Backend Development and Hosting internship. Visitors discover books, open a book to read its details, and save favourites to their own list.

What it does

  • Discover page with search by title, author or genre, plus a genre filter
  • Book details page with author, genre, rating and description
  • My Favorites page, saved separately for each visitor
